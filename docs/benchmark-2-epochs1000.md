# Benchmark 2: 1,000-epoch training-duration comparison

Longer training improves complete validation success from **2/9 to 5/9 (55.6%)** in this same-seed comparison. Episodes 006, 007 and 008 are newly successful; 004 and 005 remain successful. No model architecture, training source, optimizer setting, task rule or simulator parameter changed. Benchmark-2 test evaluation was not performed.

## Controlled training procedure

The policy was trained for **1,000 total epochs** using the original 37 successful training episodes and 6 successful validation teacher references. The original checkpoint omitted optimizer state, so training was rerun from the same seed-7 initialization. Its **first 200 epoch records exactly match the original run**, including losses and accuracy. This reproduces the baseline optimization path before adding 800 epochs; it is not a continuation with a reset optimizer. The [experiment protocol](../data/manifests/benchmark2_epochs1000_protocol.json) records input hashes and unchanged settings.

Checkpoint selection remains minimum phase-balanced supervised validation action MSE plus 0.2 times phase cross-entropy. The selected checkpoint is from **epoch 877**, not necessarily the last epoch. It was selected before measuring this run's closed-loop success.

| Measure | 200-epoch experiment | 1,000-epoch experiment |
|---|---:|---:|
| Selected epoch | 193 | 877 |
| Validation joint loss | 0.007057 | 0.003547 |
| Validation normalized action MSE | 0.006224 | 0.003380 |
| Phase accuracy on saved validation teacher states | 99.85% | 100.00% |
| Complete validation episodes | 2/9 | 5/9 |
| Mean final target-centre distance, all nine | 36.04 mm | 25.28 mm |

Normalized action MSE decreases by **45.7%**. Offline phase accuracy measures teacher states, not independently labelled phases of the learned rollouts. Improving demonstration fit translated into more complete episodes here, while substantial control failures remain.

## All-nine closed-loop evaluation

The original limits remain **10 s pushing / 30 s total**, with the same goal dwell, physical-validity checks, learned phase gating and withdrawal clearance. Approach, height, XYZ motion, full orientation commands and withdrawal are all generated by the learned recurrent policy. The evaluator supplies neither scripted Cartesian motion nor ground-truth phase.

| validation_002 | push_timeout | push_timeout | 82.09 | 19.35 |
| validation_003 | push_timeout | push_timeout | 64.35 | 18.40 |
| validation_004 | Success | Success | 7.69 | 17.65 |
| validation_005 | Success | Success | 7.78 | 16.15 |
| validation_006 | push_timeout | Success | 5.18 | 15.15 |
| validation_007 | push_timeout | Success | 5.71 | 18.00 |
| validation_008 | episode_timeout | Success | 6.10 | 17.95 |
| validation_009 | push_timeout | push_timeout | 41.50 | 19.10 |

Episode 008 now achieves complete learned withdrawal, rather than only reaching goal dwell as in the original run. Remaining failures are 001, 002, 003 and 009, all with push timeouts. Episodes 001 and 009 also have tip/table contacts (4 and 6 steps), independently violating physical validity. Episode 001 ends only 7.10 mm from the target centre but does not satisfy the full-cube containment dwell; centre distance alone is insufficient. Episode 002's final distance worsens despite the aggregate success improvement. No limits or containment thresholds were relaxed.

## Later duration comparison

The same model was subsequently trained for 2,000 epochs. Its lowest-loss checkpoint reduced validation loss further but completed only 3/9 episodes. This run's epoch-877 checkpoint therefore remains the better controller (5/9). See [the 2,000-epoch loss trends and outcomes](benchmark-2-epochs2000.md).
